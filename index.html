Frequently asked questions

Need To Know More?

How do I book a taxi?

Complete the booking form and press the WhatsApp button. Review the message and send it to make an enquiry.

Are taxi bookings available 24/7?

You can send an enquiry at any time. Actual vehicle availability and pickup times must be confirmed before booking.

Can I book an outstation or one-way trip?

You can request a one-way or round trip. Fare, route and vehicle availability need to be confirmed for your specific journey.

How much does a taxi cost?

Pricing depends on the route, vehicle, date, trip duration and other applicable charges. Ask for a fare quote before confirming.

Will I receive an instant booking confirmation?

No. The form opens WhatsApp with your trip details. Your booking is confirmed only after the details and availability are agreed.

Why might a car photo not appear?

Photos in this version load from external image links and require an internet connection. If a source is unavailable, that photo may not load.
