Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book a taxi?

Fill in the booking form and send your enquiry on WhatsApp. Discuss availability and pricing before confirming.

Can I book an outstation trip?

You can enquire about one-way, round-trip and multi-day journeys. Share your route and dates for details.

Are vehicle prices shown on this website?

Prices are not listed here. Contact us with your route and vehicle choice to discuss the fare.

Can I request a tempo traveller or mini bus?

Yes, select the vehicle in the form and send your passenger count and trip details.

Is my booking automatically confirmed?

No. WhatsApp sends your enquiry. Your booking is confirmed only after availability, fare and other details are agreed.
